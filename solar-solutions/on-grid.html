What It Is

On-Grid Solar

On-grid systems are connected to the utility grid and typically do not include battery storage.

Who it's suitable for: Properties with a reliable grid connection that want to offset grid electricity consumption.

Advantages

Advantages

  • Generally lower cost than battery-based systems
  • Simpler system design and maintenance
  • May be eligible for net-metering arrangements
  • Well suited to most urban properties
Considerations

Considerations

  • Does not provide power backup during outages
  • Depends on grid availability
  • Net-metering terms vary by local electricity board
  • Sizing should reflect actual grid usage
